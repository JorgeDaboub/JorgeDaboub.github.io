Privacy Policy — Pi Backup

Effective: November 18, 2026

Pi Backup is a personal, single-user tool. It backs up a Raspberry Pi to the owner’s own Google Drive account.

What it accesses

Pi Backup accesses Google Drive using the drive.file scope, which only permits files and folders the app itself creates. It cannot see or access any other files in the Google Drive account.

What is stored

Google authorization tokens are stored only on the owner’s own server, in the backup tool’s local configuration. No account data is sent to any other service.

Sharing and tracking

Pi Backup does not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties. It contains no analytics and no tracking.
